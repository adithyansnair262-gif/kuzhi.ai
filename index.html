KUZHI.AI

The world's most unnecessarily precise way to measure a hole in your food.

What even is this?

Kuzhi (കുഴി / குழி) simply means "hole" — the small, sacred void in the middle of a vada, an appam, a donut, or any self-respecting fried snack.

For centuries, humanity has eyeballed these holes and moved on with life.

KUZHI.AI refuses to move on. Upload a photo and our engine estimates how round the hole really is, how centered it is, and how symmetrical your specimen looks.

Built entirely for the glory of the Useless Projects category. No practical value was harmed.

A note from the founder's desk

We started KUZHI.AI after a long, heated debate over whether one vada's hole was "more round" than another's.

Science had no answer. So we built one.
